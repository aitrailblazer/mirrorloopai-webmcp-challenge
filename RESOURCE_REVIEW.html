Resulting enhancements

  • Await all nine registration promises; abort partial registration and show direct-mode fallback on failure.
  • Enforce official contract budgets, including a 1,500-character maximum for each tool result.
  • Add a 15-case expected-call corpus covering every tool, two ordered flows, ambiguity, human choice, email, cart, and payment boundaries.
  • Give judges exact Chrome DevTools instructions and distinguish deterministic corpus validation from a host-agent model evaluation.
  • Document why Firebase/Cloud Run and framework-free JavaScript remain the lowest-risk architecture.
