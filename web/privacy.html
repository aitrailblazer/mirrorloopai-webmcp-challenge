Privacy notice

Your reflection stays yours.

What we collect

If you subscribe, we store your email address, confirmation status, consent record, signup source, and a compact summary of your quiz result. We do not store your 12 individual quiz answers.

Your browser saves only the 12 choice codes on this device so you can return, review them, and change only the answers you reconsider. They are not synchronized to another device. You can remove them at any time with “Start over with blank answers” after viewing a result, or by clearing this site's browser data.

We also keep aggregate counts of quiz starts, quiz completions, and confirmed subscriptions. These analytics records contain only totals by day and overall totals. They do not contain email addresses, quiz answers, cookies, IP addresses, device fingerprints, or persistent visitor identifiers.

Why we collect it

We use it to send the reflection you requested and occasional MIRROR//LOOP updates after you confirm your email address.

Your choices

You may use the quiz without subscribing. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You may request deletion by emailing constantine@aitrailblazer.com.

Service providers and retention

Google Cloud hosts the application and database and may retain standard infrastructure security logs under its configured retention policy. The configured email-delivery provider processes messages. Pending, unconfirmed subscriptions should be deleted after 30 days; unsubscribed records should be retained only as needed to honor suppression and legal requirements.

Effective: August 29, 2026. This notice must be reviewed for the laws and launch regions selected before production release.
